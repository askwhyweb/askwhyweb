## Engineering Approach

I favour the simplest architecture that meets the need, with explicit ownership, useful observability and controlled delivery. Technical choices should be understandable to the people who operate them and proportionate to business value, risk and the cost of change. Good governance makes decisions and dependencies visible, supports teams as they deliver, and gives services clear operational owners after release.
